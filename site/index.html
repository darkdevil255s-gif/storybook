A private book · four chapters

Maybe this is my book.

Once, we wondered what the last line of a book about falling in love with someone you could never have would be.

I thought it might be:

Maybe that was all I ever wanted in the end: her hand in mine, her smile in front of me, and a few more minutes before I had to remember I could keep neither.

But maybe there was never meant to be one last line.

Maybe this is my book.
