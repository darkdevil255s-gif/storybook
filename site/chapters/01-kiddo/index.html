It never really started like a story.

There was no moment where everything suddenly made sense. No confession, no realization that clearly said, this is it. It was just there. Growing quietly somewhere inside him until one day it became too loud to ignore.

He had a name for her. Something silly, something only they understood. A name that carried everything he couldn't say out loud.

She was Kiddo.

At first, things were simple.

There was that day. She was alone at home and he went to see her. Nothing dramatic, nothing planned. Just two people who simply wanted to be around each other.

But the moment she hugged him, something shifted.

It wasn't just a hug. It was tight, warm, real. Like she didn't want to let go. Like he mattered.

And when she sat close, so close that there was no space left between them, he felt something he had never felt before.

Not excitement. Not attraction.
Something deeper.
Something dangerous.

That was the moment he lost control without even realizing it.

She probably knew, didn't she?

After that, everything about her started to matter.

The way she smiled. The way she spoke. The way she existed.

Even the smallest things became unforgettable. The smell of her hair stayed with him longer than it should have. It wasn't just a smell anymore. It became a feeling, something that pulled him in, something that made him want to stay there forever.

"I miss you.
I miss us."

This is her. Just her.

But somewhere along the way, things changed.

She started pulling away.

Not suddenly. Not in a way he could clearly point out. Just enough for him to notice. Just enough to hurt.

There were no explanations. No proper conversations. Just distance she chose and silence he couldn't understand.

And that silence started eating him from the inside.

Why are you staying away? What did I do? What am I to you?

He kept asking, sometimes directly, sometimes through long messages she would never fully respond to.

Her replies were short. Controlled. Safe. While he was pouring everything out, she was holding everything back.

That's when doubt began. Not about her. About himself.

That day kept coming back to him. The way he held her, the way he stayed close, even when she might not have wanted him to. He replayed it again and again until it started feeling wrong. Until he began to wonder if he had taken something she hadn't offered. Like he had stayed a breath longer than he was welcome.

And that thought, of being someone who made her uncomfortable, sat heavier than any rejection ever could have.

"I'm sorry."
He said it more times than he could count.

She wasn't just someone he liked.

She was someone who had once made him feel important in a way no one else ever had.

He remembered everything.

The way she used to wait for his messages. The way she would come just to see him, even if it meant doing it quietly. The way she once got angry at someone else for hurting him.

No one had ever done that for him before.

That memory alone was enough to keep him holding on.

That reel, the one where she called him her person. What do you think she meant?

And still. Even through all of it. Still her.

Eventually, he understood something that scared him even more than losing her.

"I'm getting obsessed with you."

He saw it clearly. The fear when she didn't pick up calls. The panic when he couldn't find her. The relief that hit him like something physical just by hearing her voice.

This wasn't just love anymore. It was attachment that had gone too far. And yet, even after realizing it, he couldn't step back.

So he did the only thing he could.

He told the truth.

"I love you."

Not in a perfect way. Not in a planned moment.

Just in the middle of everything. Messy, raw, too late.

He knew what it meant.

He wasn't her choice. He wasn't her future. He probably wasn't even her present.

He was just someone who stood very close to her life. Close enough to feel everything. But never close enough to belong.

"Like being next to something beautiful
that you were never meant to have."

He took every single one of these with him.

So he decided to leave.

Not because he stopped loving her. But because staying was breaking him slowly.

"I'll be gone."

Maybe distance would help. Maybe silence would finally do what conversations never could. Maybe he would learn to live without needing her.

But he didn't leave empty.

He took everything with him.

Every hug. Every smile. Every moment she made him feel seen. Even the pain.

Because that pain proved that it was real.

He hopes she forgets him.
Do you think she will?

Some people don't come into your life to stay.

They come to change you.

And she changed him completely.

There are days that become permanent without permission. You don't mark them in a diary. You don't decide to remember them. They just stay, fixed somewhere inside you like a splinter that never quite made it out. Long after everything else fades.

He doesn't expect her to remember him. In fact, he hopes she doesn't. Because forgetting him would be easier for her.

But him…

He doesn't have that choice.

He will remember her in the quiet moments. In random thoughts. In the kind of silence that feels too loud.

He doesn't know if what he felt was love exactly. Or something that used love's shape. But it was real. Whatever it was, it was real. And it was enough to last.

And sometimes, without even realizing it, he will still whisper her name the same way he always did.
